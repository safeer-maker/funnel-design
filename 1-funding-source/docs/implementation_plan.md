## The 8-Section Funnel Architecture

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Structural Rule:** This funnel uses the PAS (Problem → Agitate → Solve) framework as its psychological backbone from Section 2 onward. The hero establishes the dream outcome. Sections 2–4 are a deliberate emotional descent into the pain before the "paradigm shift" solution arrives. This contrast is what makes the solution feel *earned* rather than sold.

---

### SECTION 3 — THE PROBLEM (PAS: Problem)
**Framework Role:** Call out the pain  
**Psychology:** Pattern Interrupt + Empathy  
**Design Note:** Dark background. Heavy, serious tone. Make the merchant feel *seen*.

| Pain Point | Copy |
|---|---|
| Pain #1 | 🔴 Your cash is sitting in Stripe's reserve account — not yours. |
| Pain #2 | 🔴 Your billing software and checkout don't talk to each other, creating gaps you can't audit. |
| Pain #3 | 🔴 You were labeled "high-risk" and had your volume capped with no warning and no explanation. |
| Pain #4 | 🔴 You can't access working capital because your processor doesn't understand your business model. |
| Transition Line | *"If any of this sounds familiar — you're not alone. And it's not your fault."* |

---

### SECTION 4 — AGITATION (PAS: Agitate)
**Framework Role:** Deepen the wound before the cure  
**Psychology:** Loss Aversion — make staying with the current solution feel *more painful* than switching  
**Design Note:** No images. Statistics and hard-hitting copy only. The discomfort is intentional.

| Element | Copy |
|---|---|
| Section Headline | *"Here's What It's Actually Costing You"* |
| Stat #1 | The average merchant on flat-rate pricing overpays by **$8,000–$22,000 per year** in unnecessary fees. |
| Stat #2 | Stripe freezes or terminates **1 in 4 accounts** in "high-risk" categories within 12 months. |
| Stat #3 | Cash flow delays cost growing businesses an average of **14 days of working capital** every month. |
| Closing Agitation | *"Every day you stay with the wrong processor is a day you're subsidizing their business — not building yours."* |

---

### SECTION 5 — THE PARADIGM SHIFT (PAS: Solve — "There Is a Better Way")
**Framework Role:** The Pivot. Introduce the savior.  
**Psychology:** Relief + Hope. Color palette shifts from dark to light.  
**Design Note:** White or very light background. The visual tone *breathes* here.  

| Element | Copy |
|---|---|
| Pre-Headline | `THE ONEFUNDING DIFFERENCE` |
| Section Headline | *"What if your payment processor actually fought to keep your account alive?"* |
| Intro Paragraph | *"Most processors treat you like a transaction. OneFunding treats you like a partner. We do customized underwriting before you go live — so we know your business, your volume, and your industry. No surprises. No frozen funds. Just payments that work."* |
| 3-Column Differentiator | **Lower Rates** · **Zero Downtime** · **Industry Expertise** |

---

### SECTION 6 — HOW IT WORKS (3-Step Process)
**Framework Role:** Make it feel effortless  
**Psychology:** Reducing Perceived Effort 
**Design Note:** Cross diagonal placement of image and content

| Step | Title | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Step 1 | **Submit Your Analysis Request** | Fill out the 60-second form. Zero commitment. |
| Step 2 | **Get Your Custom Rate Report** | A Payment Expert reviews your current statement and builds a side-by-side savings breakdown. |
| Step 3 | **Switch in 24 Hours & Start Saving** | Onboarded in under 48 hours. Your first statement will tell the story. |

---

## Open Questions Before Build

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The following must be confirmed before writing a single line of code.

1. **Real testimonials available?** Do you have actual merchant testimonials with names, photos, and a business type? Or should we use anonymized placeholders flagged for replacement?
2. **Real stats available?** Can we confirm/source the Stripe freeze statistic and the average overpayment figure? These are used in Section 4 (Agitation) and need to be defensible.
3. **Form fields for the lead capture?** Minimum: Name, Business Email, Phone, Monthly Processing Volume (dropdown). Confirm or adjust.
4. **CTA destination?** Does the CTA open a calendar embed (Calendly/GHL), a pop-up form, or an inline form?
5. **Brand color palette?** Any brand guidelines or existing color preferences for the dark fintech aesthetic?
6. **Mobile-first priority?** Is traffic majority mobile (Meta ads) or desktop (LinkedIn)?

## Verification Plan

Once the user approves this plan, the build will proceed in the following order:

1. **Phase A:** Write all section copy as a plain-text script (copy-first architecture per the pre-design checklist)
2. **Phase B:** Generate visual mockup of the hero section (above-the-fold only) for approval
3. **Phase C:** Build full HTML/CSS landing page — section by section
4. **Phase D:** Run visual QA — scanability test, CTA prominence, mobile layout
