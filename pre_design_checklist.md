# Pre-Design Conversion Checklist

The biggest mistake agencies and business owners make is starting with design. High-converting landing pages are built using **Copy-First Architecture**. Use this checklist to build the foundation of your funnel before you ever open a design tool.

## Phase 1: Offer & Outcome Definition
- [ ] **Define the Dream Outcome:** What is the ultimate end-state your customer is looking for? (e.g., Not "A gym membership," but "Losing 20 lbs and feeling confident in clothes.")
- [ ] **Deconstruct the Offer:** List every single component of your offer (e.g., 24/7 support, certified coaches, meal plans).
- [ ] **Feature-to-Benefit Translation:** For every component listed above, write down the *benefit* it provides. (Feature: Weekly Check-ins -> Benefit: Never feel lost and stay completely accountable).

## Phase 2: Traffic & Strategy Alignment
- [ ] **Identify the Primary Traffic Source:**
  - *Google Ads (Search):* High-intent. Plan to lead with heavy Social Proof immediately after the Hero section.
  - *Meta/TikTok Ads (Social):* Interruption-based. Plan to lead with a Problem/Pain-Point agitation section to build the need.
- [ ] **Identify the FUDs (Fear, Uncertainty, Doubt):** List out the top 3 reasons someone might hesitate to buy from you (e.g., "What if it breaks?", "What if it takes too long?").
- [ ] **Formulate Risk Reversals:** Create guarantees or policies to counter those FUDs (e.g., 30-day money-back guarantee, no hidden fees, cancel anytime).

## Phase 3: Copywriting (The Blueprint)
*Note: Write this in a plain text document. Do not worry about design yet.*

- [ ] **Write the Hero Headline:** (End Result + Timeframe + Emotional Payoff). *Must be completely understandable even if it's the only thing they read.*
- [ ] **Write the Sub-Headline:** (How you deliver the result + unique differentiator).
- [ ] **Draft the Value Proposition Headlines:** Write 4 to 8 highly scannable headlines that fuse your features with benefits. 
- [ ] **Map out the "How it Works" Steps:** Condense your customer onboarding or delivery process into 3 or 4 incredibly simple steps.

## Phase 4: Asset Collection (Proof & Emotion)
- [ ] **Gather Verifiable Social Proof:** Do not use plain text reviews. Collect:
  - High-quality photos of the customers.
  - Full names and locations (if applicable).
  - Screenshots of reviews from verified platforms (Google, Trustpilot).
  - Video testimonials (if available).
- [ ] **Audit "Featured On" Logos (If applicable):** If you are using publication logos, find the exact quote or context of *what* they said about you to place next to the logo.
- [ ] **Source Emotional Imagery/Video:** 
  - Find or shoot imagery that shows the *after state* (smiling, relieved, successful customers).
  - Ensure imagery visually demonstrates the core value proposition (e.g., if you are a family-run business, show a warm picture of your actual team, not sterile stock photos).

## Phase 5: Design Strategy
- [ ] **Map Copy to Layouts:** Review your written copy and decide the best way to display it visually. (e.g., If the copy is a step-by-step process, plan for a horizontal timeline layout. If it's a comparison, plan for a vs. table).
- [ ] **Establish Visual Hierarchy Rules:** Decide on your typography scale. The headline must be the most prominent, the sub-headline secondary, and the CTA button must be a contrasting, eye-catching color used nowhere else on the page.

> [!TIP]
> Only once every single box above is checked should you begin wireframing or designing your landing page in a page builder.
