To truly understand what this client does, you have to look past the surface of "credit card processing."

### **What is their actual Business Model?**

### **Who is their Target Audience?**

1. **High-Risk / Regulated Industries:** By highlighting **LegitScript**, "Stigma Supplements", and "Avera MD" (Healthcare), they are aggressively targeting the nutraceutical, telehealth, CBD, and supplement spaces. These businesses struggle to find reliable payment processors.
2. **Global & High-Tech Commerce:** Offering Stablecoin/Crypto settlements and "Agentic Commerce" appeals to forward-thinking SaaS, AI startups, and international marketplaces.
3. **High-Volume Traditional Businesses:** Restaurants, retail, and B2B software companies who want lower rates (Interchange-Plus pricing) rather than flat-rate fees.

### **The Competitive Advantage**

Their distinct edge is **Payment Orchestration**. By integrating with multiple backend gateways (NMI, USA ePay, Authorize.net), if one banking partner goes down or rejects a transaction, OneFunding can route the payment through another gateway. This ensures 99.9% uptime and protects merchants from sudden account closures.
